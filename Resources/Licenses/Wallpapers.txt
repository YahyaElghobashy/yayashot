YayaShot bundled wallpapers (Resources/Backgrounds/mac)

The seven images shipped as "macOS" wallpapers in upstream BetterShot had no
recorded license or source. YayaShot replaced them on 2026-09-27 with original
abstract gradients generated locally with ImageMagick (Shepards sparse-color
interpolation and a Gaussian blur). File names were kept so saved projects keep
resolving their background.
